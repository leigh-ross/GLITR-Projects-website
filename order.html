How to Order

Custom piñatas and websites made exactly to your needs. Here is how we bring your idea to life.

1. Get in Touch

Fill out the form below. Let us know your event date (or website launch goal), your budget, and what you have in mind.

2. Design & Quote

We will discuss the design, size, and details. Once we agree on the plan, we'll send you a final quote and timeline.

3. Deposit & Build

To secure your spot in our schedule, a 50% deposit is required. Once received, we start building!

4. Handover

We will arrange pickup/delivery for a piñata, or launch your website. The remaining balance is due upon completion.
